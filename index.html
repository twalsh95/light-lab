What Light Lab saves

Your current setup is kept in this browser only, so it's still here next time on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no sign-in.

To keep a copy, use Save PNG or Save PDF for the picture, diagram and settings, or Save setup for a file you can open again later.
