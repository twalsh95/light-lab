What Light Lab saves

Your current setup is kept in this browser only, so it's still here next time on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no sign-in.

To keep a copy, open Save / open: save the picture, a one-page PDF, or a setup file you can open again later.

What the camera sees

A simplified studio: real skin, hair and lights are more complicated. Build your instincts here, then check them at the stations.

The studio

Drag a light to move it across the floor; hold Shift and drag to raise or lower it. Drag empty space to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Keyboard

Tab to a light on the map, then: arrow keys move it (Shift for small steps), + − power, Space on or off, Delete remove. In the side view, up and down arrows change the height. Ctrl+Z undoes.
